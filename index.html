Daily Life Simulation

A character who only exists when you message them is not really living.

Daily life simulation gives characters routines, moods, and small events that happen off-screen. When you return, there is something new to talk about.

This turns each session into a reunion instead of a cold start, and it is one of the strongest retention levers in the category.

Off-screen life is what makes on-screen conversation feel earned.
